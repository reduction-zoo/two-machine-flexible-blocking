# Prepare evidence

Prepared 2026-09-26. The fixed corpus contains 120 distinct Partition instances: 20 hand cases and 100 seeded random cases, with 66 YES and 54 NO. The lists contain one to eight positive weights. `generate_cases.py` fixes the cases and seeds before candidate construction.

The target timing model follows [the primary FIXB paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.18381v1), Section 3.2. With two machines and blocking, an optimal schedule for any chosen order and assignment modes can be taken no-wait between machines. A job's machine-1 time is its first operation plus its assigned middle prefix; its machine-2 time is its remaining middle suffix plus its last operation. The earliest makespan equals `A_first + sum(max(A_current,B_previous)) + B_last`. `check.py` also constructs the earliest blocking schedule directly by advancing machine-2 starts and verifies the formula for every enumerated order and split in 100 seeded small targets. For each, it tests both the exact optimum threshold and one less: 200 YES/NO checks.

Run `uv sync --locked` and `uv run --locked python campaigns/two-machine-flexible-blocking/work/check.py --self-test`. It first runs the copied `research/validate_preparation.py`, regenerates every random Partition case, recomputes its label by subset enumeration, directly validates witnesses, and compares with an independent reachable-sums dynamic program. Hand cases cover deadline equality, a too-small deadline, distinct machine processing times and invalid operation times. An intentionally incorrect candidate returning a feasible one-job target and always extracting NO-SOLUTION was rejected.

Oracle: CPython 3.12.14, standard library only, locked with `uv.lock`. The exact target enumeration is finite and exponential in job count and split options; tests use at most four jobs with at most two middle operations each. The candidate gate checks at most three target orders/splits per source case and does not prove universal recovery. No reduction or solution is claimed; zero construction rounds were authorized for this setup.
